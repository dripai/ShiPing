# ShiPing Privacy Policy

[简体中文](PRIVACY.md)

Effective date: October 1, 2026

ShiPing is a local screen recorder for Windows, macOS, and Linux. This policy covers recordings, audio, and settings. The independent project preserves the existing data-handling behavior.

## Data handling

- Screen, window, or region pixels are read only when you start recording. System audio and microphone input are read when you enable them.
- Pixels and audio are used only to produce the local MP4 file you request. GIF recordings do not contain audio.
- Recordings stay in the local folder you select; settings stay in the operating system's application-data directory.
- No account is required. ShiPing does not collect identity, contacts, payment data, or advertising identifiers, and has no advertising, behavioral analytics, or telemetry uploads.
- Recordings, audio, and settings are not uploaded to developer servers or third-party analytics services. You control any file sharing or GitHub feedback you initiate.

## Your choices and contact

You can cancel recording, disable audio, change settings, and delete local files or settings. Do not include private content in public GitHub feedback; feedback is subject to GitHub's privacy policy and terms.

Updates will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dripai/ShiPing/issues).
